Unit 5 — Algebra

A letter is a number nobody has told you yet, and an equation is a claim you can test. This unit keeps both of those physical for as long as possible: cups hide the unknown, and a balance decides whether a claim is still true. The rule about doing the same to both sides is never asserted here — students break it on purpose and watch the beam drop.

20 instructional days 4 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.EE standards
