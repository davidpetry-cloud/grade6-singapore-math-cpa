Unit 1 — Whole Numbers & Order of Operations

Sixth grade opens by rebuilding place value as a system rather than a set of names, then uses that system to justify why order of operations exists at all. Every lesson runs concrete, then pictorial, then abstract.

10 instructional days 2 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.NS · 6.EE standards
