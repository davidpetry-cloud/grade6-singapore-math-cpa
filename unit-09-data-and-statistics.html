Unit 9 — Data Analysis & Statistics

A dot plot, a histogram, and the mean and median of a dataset are three views of one thing, and this unit keeps them moving together rather than teaching them as separate topics. Drag the bin-width slider on a real outlier and watch it vanish into a bar; drag the outlier itself and watch the mean chase it while the median holds still. Both are arguments a printed chart cannot make.

15 instructional days 3 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.SP standards
