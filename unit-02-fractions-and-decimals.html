Unit 2 — Fractions & Decimals

Fractions and decimals are two notations for one idea: a whole cut into equal pieces. This unit builds both from the same pictures, so that dividing by a fraction stops being a rule to remember and becomes a question a student can act out with paper. The reciprocal is derived, never handed over.

20 instructional days 4 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.NS standards
