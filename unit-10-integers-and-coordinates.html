Unit 10 — Negative Numbers & the Coordinate Plane

The final unit of the year, and the one that ties the loose thread from Unit 6 back in: polygons on a grid, now that the grid itself — negative numbers, absolute value, all four quadrants — has been properly built first. A submarine's depth, a debt, a temperature below freezing: negative numbers get a reason to exist before they get a rule.

13 instructional days 3 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.NS & 6.G standards
