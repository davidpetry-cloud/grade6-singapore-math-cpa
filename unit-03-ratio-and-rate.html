Unit 3 — Ratio & Rate

A ratio is a relationship that survives when both amounts change together. This unit builds that idea from grouping counters up to scale drawings, and treats the unit rate not as a division rule but as a step back along a line to the value of one — the move that makes any two rates comparable.

15 instructional days 3 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.RP standards
