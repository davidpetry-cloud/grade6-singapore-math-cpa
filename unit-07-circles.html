Unit 7 — Circles

π is not handed over in this unit — it is measured. Students wrap string around tins for two days, divide circumference by diameter, and find the same number coming back from a coin and a bucket alike. The area formula is built the same way, by cutting a circle into wedges and fanning them into a shape they already know how to measure. The scatter in their measurements is kept, not tidied away: a pattern that survives messy data is one worth believing.

13 instructional days 3 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.G standards
