Unit 8 — Volume of Solids

Boxes get filled with cubes before any formula appears, and nets get folded before they are judged. The folding tool exists for one reason paper cannot manage: when a net fails, it names which of the three conditions was broken, so a student learns why rather than just that. Volume and surface area are kept deliberately side by side, because the box is the same and only the question changes.

15 instructional days 3 weeks 55 minutes per lesson 6.G standards
