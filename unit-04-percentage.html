Unit 4 — Percentage

Percent is not a fourth kind of number. It is the rate from Unit 3 with the second quantity fixed at one hundred, drawn on the hundredths grid from Unit 2. The question that runs through every lesson here is the same one: a percent of what? Most errors in this unit are answers to that question rather than to the arithmetic.
