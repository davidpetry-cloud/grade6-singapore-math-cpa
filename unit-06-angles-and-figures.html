Unit 6 — Angles & Geometric Figures

Every unknown angle in this unit comes with a reason, not just a number — torn paper corners lining up into a straight edge, a fold that maps one base angle exactly onto the other, a diagonal that turns any quadrilateral into two triangles. The coordinate-plane strand of this standard now lives in Unit 10, once integers and plotting are in place; this unit stays with reasoning about shapes themselves.
